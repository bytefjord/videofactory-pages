Privacy Policy — VideoFactory uploader

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What this app is

VideoFactory uploader is a personal desktop application. It is not offered to the public, does not have user accounts, and does not collect data from other people.

Data access

The app requests the YouTube OAuth scope youtube.force-ssl to access the YouTube Data API v3 on behalf of its owner's own Google account. This access is used only to upload and manage videos (including metadata, thumbnails, captions and playlists) on YouTube channels owned by the app's owner.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never transmitted to any server other than Google's API endpoints. The app does not collect, store or share any personal data of third parties.
